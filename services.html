Our Services

Professional arborist consulting services backed by decades of experience and industry-leading certifications.

Tree Risk Assessment

Our comprehensive tree risk assessments evaluate the health, structural integrity, and potential hazards of trees on your property. Using industry-standard protocols, we identify risks and provide clear recommendations to protect people and property.

What We Evaluate

  • Structural defects and decay
  • Root system stability
  • Branch attachment and crown condition
  • Site factors (soil, drainage, exposure)
  • Target assessment (structures, high-traffic areas)

Ideal For

  • Homeowners concerned about mature trees near homes
  • Property managers maintaining safe landscapes
  • Pre-purchase property inspections
  • Post-storm damage evaluation
Request an Assessment

Tree & Landscape Appraisal

Accurate valuation of trees and landscaping for insurance claims, property transactions, tax purposes, and legal matters. Our appraisals follow recognized industry standards and are backed by detailed documentation.

Common Applications

  • Insurance claims for tree damage or loss
  • Real estate transactions and property valuations
  • Estate and tax assessments
  • Legal disputes and expert witness testimony
  • Construction damage claims

Our Approach

  • Site inspection and tree measurements
  • Species-specific valuation factors
  • Condition and location adjustments
  • Detailed written reports with supporting documentation
Request an Appraisal

Tree Inventory Services

Comprehensive tree inventories for municipalities, HOAs, commercial properties, and large estates. We provide the data and insights you need for effective tree management, budgeting, and long-term planning.

What's Included

  • Species identification and location mapping
  • Tree measurements (DBH, height, crown spread)
  • Condition ratings and maintenance recommendations
  • Risk assessment for high-priority trees
  • Database and GIS-compatible data formats

Benefits

  • Data-driven budgeting for tree care
  • Prioritized maintenance schedules
  • Compliance with municipal requirements
  • Long-term canopy management planning
