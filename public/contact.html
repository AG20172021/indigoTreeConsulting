Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Indianapolis and the surrounding central Indiana region. For larger projects, we may travel further — contact us to discuss your location.

How quickly can you schedule an assessment?

We typically schedule assessments within 1-2 weeks, though this can vary based on season and current workload. Urgent matters are prioritized.

Do you provide tree care services like pruning or removal?

We specialize in consulting and assessment services. We do not perform tree work ourselves, which allows us to provide unbiased recommendations. We can refer you to reputable tree care companies and provide specifications for their work.

What should I expect during a tree risk assessment?

We'll inspect the tree(s) in question, evaluate structural integrity, site conditions, and potential targets. You'll receive a written report with findings and recommendations within a few business days.

Are your appraisals accepted by insurance companies?

Yes, our appraisals follow industry standards and are prepared by a Registered Consulting Arborist. They are widely accepted by insurance companies, attorneys, and courts.
