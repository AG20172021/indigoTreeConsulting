About Us

Indigo Tree Consulting combines scientific expertise with practical experience to deliver trusted arborist services.

Andrew Mertz

Founder & Chief Operating Officer

Registered Consulting Arborist #542 Board Certified Master Arborist

Indigo Tree Consulting, LLC was founded by Andrew Mertz in 2016. Andrew is a Registered Consulting Arborist and Board Certified Master Arborist with over 18 years of professional arboricultural and restoration ecology experience.

He is also a graduate of the School of Public and Environmental Affairs at Indiana University with a Master of Public Affairs.

Andrew's professional experience includes over a decade of public service with the City of Indianapolis as a restoration ecologist and arborist for Indy Parks and Recreation. He served as Urban Forestry Manager for the City of Indianapolis from 2010 to 2012.

From 2012 to 2016, Andrew worked as a consulting arborist for a locally owned tree care company. In that role, he prepared tree and landscape appraisals, tree risk assessments, municipal tree inventories, and specifications for tree care services including tree removal, tree pruning, and plant health care.
